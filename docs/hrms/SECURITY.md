## Ongoing controls
- **Audit log:** an append-only `hr_audit_log` covers settings, roles, employees, PII (redacted), and documents, assets, exit, payroll and other sensitive tables. Only users with `audit.view` can read it.
- **Payroll separation:** payroll permissions belong to `payroll_admin` and `super_admin`. HR admins cannot read salary data.
- **PII split:** personal fields live in `hr_employee_private`. They are readable only by the employee and by `employees.view_sensitive`.
- **Private storage:** documents and avatars live in private buckets. Downloads use 60-second signed URLs and are logged in `hr_document_access_log`.
- **Honest outcomes:** the UI shows success only after the server confirms. Email shows its real outbox status.

## Remaining risks / owner decisions
- **Out of HRMS scope:** these legacy Express endpoints are still unauthenticated: `/api/inquiries`, `/api/schedule/*` and `/api/portal/*` (Client Portal). Retire them or protect them before hosting `server/`.
- **Client Portal:** it still relied on the removed demo login. It now needs Supabase-backed client accounts, which are not part of this assignment.
- **Production rollout:** see `DEPLOYMENT.md` §6.
